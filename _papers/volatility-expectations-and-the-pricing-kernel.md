coauthors: with Aaron Huberman

abstract: >-
  We document that the curvature of the conditional pricing kernel estimated from
  S&P 500 index options falls by roughly half after 2008, and we link this break to a shift
  in investors' volatility and tail beliefs measured from survey data. We develop an
  Epstein-Zin economy with variance-biased, persistent beliefs to account for the pre- and
  post-2008 shapes of the kernel.
